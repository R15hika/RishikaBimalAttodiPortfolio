Project Overview ♡

  • A 2D point-and-click game set in a zoo at night: keep four animals fed, healthy and sane until your shift ends at 6 am.
  • Runs on a custom engine we wrote from scratch: ECS, renderer, physics, audio (FMOD), scenes and prefabs in JSON, and an ImGui editor.
  • Made by NocturnalStudios, 6 programmers and 2 designers (RTIS + UXGD), across six milestones.

My Contribution ♡

  • Graphics systems & rendering (M1–M5): sprites with GLM transforms, prefab / JSON-driven rendering, the 2D camera, and scene-level VFX with a particle system.
  • A layering system shared by graphics, logic, physics and collision, with drag-to-reorder in the editor and save/load for layered scenes.
  • Engine setup (M1): project scaffolding, CMake build pipeline, Visual Studio solution generation and third-party libraries for the team.
  • Gameplay & tools: scene switching, play/exit state flow, toy hide/reveal, FPS display, editor drag-and-drop, and the Windows installer (M3, M6).

Experience Gained ♡

  • Building a renderer and engine systems from scratch instead of relying on Unity or Unreal.
  • Designing systems that other programmers build on, like layering, prefabs and the build pipeline.
  • Working closely with artists and designers so their assets and ideas run well in the engine.
  • Shipping a game on schedule across six milestones in an 8-person team.
