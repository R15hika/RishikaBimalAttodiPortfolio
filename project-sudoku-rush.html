Multiplayer Sudoku Rush ♡

Feature ·

A real-time multiplayer Sudoku race for 2–3 players on a LAN: everyone solves the same board and the server decides who claimed each cell first. Built from scratch in C++ over UDP with a custom reliability layer, with SQLite accounts and a Win32 client. I owned the backend and gameplay.

Project Overview ♡

  • A competitive Sudoku race (inspired by usdoku.com): 2–3 players fill the same puzzle at once and score by who solves each cell first.
  • Server-authoritative: clients only send input; the server validates every move and owns the scores, so play stays fair and in sync.
  • Runs over UDP with sequence numbers and ACKs to cope with packet loss, reordering and duplicates on a LAN.

My Contribution ♡

  • The Sudoku game logic on the server: puzzle generation, answer checking, cell ownership and hints.
  • The scoring and live leaderboard: solve-order points, wrong-guess penalties and ranking after every move.
  • The server-side room and match system: public/private rooms, ready-up, match start, round end and play again.
  • Match history and SQLite integration (accounts, results, friends), plus the chat and social features: room chat, private messages and friend requests.

Experience Gained ♡

  • Designing server-authoritative game state so several players stay in sync with one source of truth.
  • Managing many rooms and players at once on one server (HostEngine and RoomDirectory).
  • Storing and querying results reliably with SQLite and structured queries.
  • Splitting work in a two-person team: agreeing on packets and interfaces between my backend and my teammate's client and networking.
